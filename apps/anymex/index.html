01 About

AnymeX is an open-source, cross-platform app for streaming anime and reading manga, manhwa, light and web novels, with deep integration for tracking services (AniList, MyAnimeList, Simkl and Kitsu). Extensions keep sources working, and your library, progress and statistics stay in sync across services.

Device compatibility: choose a profile on the catalog page.

02 Install with

Pick your client. Links are generated from the catalog on every build — never hard-coded.

KsignCompatibleTap to add the source to Ksign. If nothing happens, open Ksign sources and paste the URL.
