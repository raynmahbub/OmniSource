01 About

TwitchAdBlock is an open-source tweak for the native Twitch iOS app that blocks advert break requests at the network layer, so streams play without ads and without the purple loading screen. This fork by gunnerkidBT keeps the tweak working on the latest Twitch releases (29.x-30.x) and modern iOS versions, and publishes ready-to-sideload IPAs built from the official tweak source.

Device compatibility: choose a profile on the catalog page.

Upstream publishes no screenshots for this app, and OmniSource does not invent them.

Twitch 31.7 | TwitchAdBlock 0.1.15

**TwitchMods v0.1.15**, for Twitch iOS **31.7**

### New: see what moderators delete
Turn on **Keep Deleted Messages** in Settings → Emotes. When a moderator deletes a message, or times out or bans someone, their messages **stay in chat** instead of disappearing, and a short note like **"🛡 ronni was timed out for 10m"** shows up so you know what happened.

Turn on **Hide "From Channels You Follow"** in Settings → Home to remove the row of clips and recaps above the live channels on the Live page.

- The **"Go Ad-Free"** button on the Following tab is hidden again. Twitch 31.7 rebuilt that tab, so the old way of hiding it stopped working.

Everything from v0.1.14 is still included: Twitch 31.7 support, ad-blocking, 7TV / BTTV / FFZ emotes (send them with the 7TV bar or by holding the emote button), Keep Live Feed Playing, Hide Stories and your default launch screen.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Twitch 31.7 is a major rebuild of the app.** Twitch rewrote big parts of it, including **Home, the Live feed, chat and the stream player**, using a new framework (React Native). Your account may get the new versions or the old ones, depending on Twitch's rollout.
>
> **This release updates every TwitchMods feature to work with both.** Older TwitchMods versions won't work properly with Twitch 31.7, so update to this one.

**TwitchMods v0.1.14**, for Twitch iOS **31.7**

### Updated for Twitch 31.7
All of these were checked on Twitch 31.7, on both the new and old parts of the app:
- **Ad-blocking**: live and VOD pre-rolls, feed ads
- **3rd-party emotes**: 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ, in both the new and the old chat
- **Keep Live Feed Playing**: the Live feed no longer cuts a stream off with the Watch/Follow overlay
- **Hide Twitch Stories**
- **Default launch screen**: Twitch 31.7 now picks its own starting tab, and TwitchMods makes sure yours wins
- **Following tab ad cleanup**
- **Fixed:** the app crashing when you scrolled down to TwitchMods in Settings

### New: send 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes
- Tap into chat and a **7TV** bar appears above the keyboard. In Twitch's older chat it's a 7TV button next to the emote button instead.
- You can also **press and hold Twitch's own emote button** to open the picker.
- The picker shows the channel's emotes plus the global ones. **Animated emotes move**, there's **search**, and your recent picks show at the top. Tap an emote and it's typed into your message.
- Don't want the bar? Turn on **Hide 7TV Bar** in Settings → Emotes. Press and hold still works.
- Heads up: your *own* messages show the emote as a word on your screen, but everyone else using 7TV, BTTV or FFZ sees the picture.

Release notes
Twitch 29.4 | TwitchAdBlock 29.4.2

## Highlights

- **Launch Screen picker** — choose which tab Twitch opens to. Includes Home sub-tabs (Following / Live / Clips) and Browse sub-tabs (Categories / Live Channels), plus Activity and Profile.
- **Hide Twitch Stories** — toggle to remove the horizontal Stories strip at the top of Home.
- **Default-proxy reachability indicator** in TwitchMods settings — live Online / Offline / Checking status.
- **New banner blocking** — McDonalds-style brand offers (`OfferPromotion`, `PromotionDisplay`) and the in-app "buy Bits" prompt (`BitsProductPromotion`) are removed from Following / Home responses.
- **TwitchMods rebrand** — user-facing strings (settings entry, screen title, version footer) now read "TwitchMods". Internal symbols, keys, and dylib filename unchanged.
- **AdBlock proxy on by default** for fresh installs.

## Bug fixes

- **Live & Clips tabs broken** after the previous release — the recursive GraphQL strip was orphaning edges in `feedItems.edges`. Now edge-aware (removes the whole edge when `edge.node.__typename` matches), and skips response re-serialization when nothing was actually stripped (was confusing Apollo's cache).
- Speculative ad typenames (`AdProperties`, `PromotedContent`, `SponsoredContent`, etc.) removed from the blocklist — several were appearing as metadata fields on legitimate `Stream` / `Clip` objects.

## Robustness / quality

- Subdomain-aware ad host matching (now blocks `*.amazon-adsystem.com`).
- LRU eviction for the emote registry (capped at 50 channels).
- IRC emote positions counted as Unicode grapheme clusters instead of UTF-16 units — emoji no longer shift the rendered offsets.
- Recursive `__typename` scan logs unknown ad-like values once per `(operation, typename)`, so new banners surface in logs without code archaeology.
- `twab_warnIfClassMissing` runs at startup for every Swift hook target — silent feature regressions on a Twitch version bump now produce a visible warning.
- Typed `TWABKey*` constants instead of `@"..."` literals — typos become compile errors.
- Atomic synthetic-emote-ID generator (one less queue hop per registration).
- WebSocket handler idempotent (associated-object marker) + non-IRC frames short-circuit before parsing.
- Settings cells properly reuse via `dequeueReusableCellWithIdentifier:`.
- Proxy address field saves on every keystroke (not just end-editing).
- Deprecated `[tweakDefaults synchronize]` calls removed.
